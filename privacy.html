Pack 828 App privacy notice

Effective November 17, 2026

The Pack 828 App is run by the volunteer leaders of Cub Scout Pack 828 in Conway, South Carolina, chartered by Friendship Missionary Baptist Church. It helps our families and leaders keep up with the pack calendar, announcements, to-dos, scout accounts, advancement and expenses. This page explains, in plain language, what the app keeps and who can see it.

The short version: the app only holds what the pack already keeps for running the pack. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and families only see their own scouts.

What the app keeps

  • Your name and email address, and which scouts your account is linked to. The pack adds these; nobody can sign in unless a leader has approved their email.
  • About each scout: name, rank, advancement (adventures, ranks, awards and the requirements signed off, copied from Scoutbook Plus), meeting sign-in history from the pack's sign-in sheet, and the scout account balance from the pack treasurer's ledger.
  • For leaders: announcements and to-dos they post, adventures they log, and expenses they submit, including photos of receipts.

Signing in

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google. If you use Google, Google tells the app your name, email address, and that your email is verified. The app uses that only to confirm who you are and match you to the pack roster. It does not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Your phone keeps a sign-in key and a copy of the last information it loaded, so the app opens quickly and works without a signal. Signing out removes both from that phone.

Who can see what

  • Families see their own linked scouts' balances, advancement and sign-ins, plus the pack calendar, announcements and to-dos meant for them.
  • Leaders also see advancement and sign-ins for all scouts, so they can run dens and record progress.
  • The treasurer and Cubmaster also see every scout's balance and all submitted expenses and receipts.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored in the pack's own Google Workspace account (a Google Sheet and a Google Drive folder), and the app's server runs on Google Apps Script in that account. The app's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see basic connection details such as your IP address when the page loads. Emails the app sends (sign-in codes, and expense alerts to the treasurer) go out through the pack's Google account.

Children

Accounts are for parents, guardians and adult leaders. Cub Scouts don't sign in. Information about a scout comes from the pack's own records and from Scoutbook Plus, and is shown only to that scout's family and to pack leaders as described above.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade any of this information, and we don't share it outside the pack's leaders except where the law requires it.

Changes, removal and questions

To correct something, remove a login, or ask what the app holds about you or your scout, contact the Cubmaster at cubmaster@pack828sc.org or 843-315-4475. If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top.
